# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list accurate. Law and platform terms change often, so corrections are as welcome as new entries.

## What belongs here

- Primary sources: statutes, court decisions, official agency guidance, and the tools' own terms of service.
- Practical resources that help AI creators understand or protect their rights: guides from public bodies, bar associations, universities, and established organisations.
- Services and tools, **including commercial ones and competitors of the maintainers**, described neutrally, with their limits.

## What does not belong here

- Entries without a source. Every factual statement needs a link to where it can be checked.
- Marketing copy. Describe what a service does and what it does not do; no superlatives.
- Summaries of real disputes involving private individuals that are not already public court records.

## Format

- One entry per line: `- [Name](https://link) - Description.` The description starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop.
- Put the entry in the most specific section. Keep the order used in that section (by jurisdiction, then date).
- For laws, decisions and terms of service, add the date of the version you read, e.g. `(terms updated 2026-03-01)`.
- Update the **Last verified** date of a section when you have re-checked it.

## Corrections

If an entry is out of date or wrong, open an issue or a pull request with the source that shows the change. Wrong information is removed quickly; please do not wait to be sure it is worth reporting.

## Not legal advice

Contributions are information, not legal advice. Do not phrase entries as advice to a particular person.
